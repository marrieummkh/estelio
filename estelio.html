LEAD GENERATION

More Than Leads.

Real conversations with motivated buyers & sellers.

At Estelio, we combine targeted digital advertising with a hands-on qualification process to help real estate professionals connect with homeowners and buyers who are genuinely interested in making a move.

1

Targeted Advertising

We launch targeted campaigns across Google, Facebook, and Meta based on the specific market, location, and type of prospect our real estate partners want to reach.

Our campaigns may focus on offers such as home valuations, property appraisals, selling opportunities, or buyers actively searching for properties.

2

Prospect Responds

When a homeowner or buyer interacts with one of our ads, they provide their contact information and details about their real estate needs.

This gives us the initial information needed to determine whether the prospect could be a potential opportunity for one of our realtor partners.

3

Initial Verification

We don't simply pass raw information to a realtor. Our ISAs and sales agents contact each prospect to verify the information they submitted and confirm that the person is genuinely interested in buying or selling.

We verify key details, including their property information, motivation, and current situation.

4

Quality & Intent Verification

Once the initial information has been verified, our management team conducts an additional verification call. We speak directly with the prospect to better understand:

    This additional layer of vetting helps ensure the realtor receives more than just a name and phone number.

    5

    Appointment Scheduling

    When a prospect is qualified and willing to speak with a realtor, we coordinate a specific time for the conversation.

    The realtor receives the prospect's information along with the scheduled call time, allowing them to reach out when the prospect is expecting their call.

    6

    Follow-Up & Rescheduling

    Real estate prospects don't always answer on the first attempt.

    If a prospect misses the scheduled call, our team can follow up and work to reschedule the conversation rather than simply marking the lead as unsuccessful.
